How to play

  • ← → (or A D, or L R) to move. Space/↑ to jump. You hop up small ledges automatically.
  • 💧 Water drips and a blue lane show where ice is about to fall. Don't be under it!
  • 🧊 Blocks pile up – climb over them. The higher you get, the more points.
  • 🪙 Collect coins to buy new monsters & power-ups.
  • 👶 Rescue lost babies – they follow you and give bonus points. Protect them!
